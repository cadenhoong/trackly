The why

Your fitness is scattered across five apps.

The problem

Tracking shouldn’t feel like a second job.

  • Calories in one app, steps in another, sleep in a third.
  • Generic macro targets that ignore whether today is a training day.
  • You log everything but never find out if the week actually worked.

The fix

Trackly puts it all in one place.

  • One home screen for calories, macros, steps, sleep and weigh-ins.
  • Protein at 1g per lb and carbs that rise on workout days.
  • A weekly grade with a clear tip to improve, or to stay the course.
